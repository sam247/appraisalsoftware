# appraisal.software — Signature dot

- `wordmark.svg`: ink wordmark and jade dot, transparent background.
- `wordmark-white.svg`: white wordmark and jade dot for dark backgrounds.
- `mark.svg` / `mark-white.svg`: standalone single-storey `a.` mark.
- `app-icon.svg`: white `a.` and jade dot on an ink rounded square.
- `icon-16.png`, `icon-32.png`, `icon-48.png`: browser-size exports.
- `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`: app and sharing exports.
- Next.js site icons: `src/app/icon.svg`, `src/app/favicon.ico` (16/32/48), and `src/app/apple-icon.png` (180).

Colours: jade `#29A46C`, ink `#171714`, warm white `#FAF9F6`.
Wordmark lettering uses outlined Inter shapes; no installed fonts are required.
Keep the original proportions, preserve the jade dot, and use the white version on dark backgrounds. Give the logo at least the dot's diameter of clear space. Use the square app icon at small sizes instead of shrinking the full wordmark.
